About

I’m a GIS and urban data analyst based in Madrid, currently completing a Master’s in Smart and Sustainable Cities at the Universidad Complutense de Madrid. My work spans geospatial analysis for urban mobility and environmental sustainability, international policy research that has fed into European Commission submissions, and academic work on urban resilience, climate risk, and city governance.

I’m bilingual in English and Spanish and comfortable presenting technical spatial analysis to specialist and non-specialist audiences alike. I’m currently looking for GIS, urban mobility, or smart-cities roles in Madrid and across the EU.
